## 0. The frame

Two channels. Two voices. One dignity filter.

| | **RomaliteHQ** | **Veronica (personal)** |
|---|---|---|
| Who it's for | Families with adults with Down syndrome; people who want to see Erica be Erica | Architect mothers — women running quiet operations the world doesn't notice |
| Who's on camera | Erica is the judge. Family is the setup. Paired format. | Veronica, mostly alone. Voice-led, reflective. |
| What it sells | The Daily Planner, GiGi's/DSNetwork community trust, future RomaliteHQ products | Veronica's frameworks, print shop, Etsy, eventual courses/guides for caregiver-operators |
| Tone | Real, unscripted, deadpan-funny, dignified | Plain, direct, structural. No inspiration porn. |
| Format center of gravity | Short-form vertical (Shorts), captured in the moment | Long-form (8–15 min), one strong upload a week |
| Cadence | 3–4 Shorts/week + 1 monthly compilation | 1 long video/week. Saturn pace. |
| The rule | Don't coach the reaction. | Don't perform the answer. |

**The dignity filter (both channels):**
1. No inspiration porn. No "look how brave/strong/inspiring" framing.
2. No retakes to get a "better" reaction or a "better" tear.
3. No content where the subject is the object lesson. Erica is the protagonist. Veronica is the builder, not the survivor.
4. If you wouldn't say it to a peer over coffee, don't say it to camera.

## 1. RomaliteHQ YouTube

### 1.1 Mission

Show what life with Erica is actually like — funny, sharp, opinionated, ordinary — so other families recognize themselves and so the broader audience sees an adult with Down syndrome as the lead character of her own life. The Daily Planner is the operational backbone you can see in the background; it's never the subject.

### 1.2 Content pillars (six)

All six are already mapped to the 40 ideas you sent. Each pillar gets a playlist on the channel.

| Pillar | What it is | Source ideas |
|---|---|---|
| **At Home with Gonzo** | Erica judges Dad. Low-effort, high-frequency. | #1–10 |
| **EriEats** | Restaurants, drive-thrus, first bites, ratings. | #11–17 |
| **EriPOV** | Meta-glasses POV of a real day. | #18–24 |
| **Erica Picks** | Erica is the decider. Outfit, movie, restaurant, playlist. | #25–29 |
| **Family Dynamics** | Brothers, grandson, old family videos. | #30–33 |
| **Quiet Capture** | No setup. Real. The mirror, the dog, the hands working. | #34–40 |

### 1.3 Format spec

- **Length:** 15–45 seconds for Shorts. Compilations (monthly) 6–10 minutes.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical. Shoot vertical, full stop.
- **Captions:** Always burned in. Erica's voice is the lead, captions are accessibility.
- **Hook:** First 1.5 seconds is Erica's face or Erica's reaction — not a title card, not a question.
- **End screen:** RomaliteHQ logo + "Daily Planner link in bio" on every Short.
- **The rule again:** if you asked for a retake, cut it.

### 1.4 KPIs

In priority order:
1. **Returning viewers / subscriber loyalty.** This is a long-tail community, not a viral channel.
2. **Comment quality.** Other DS families saying "this is us."
3. **Daily Planner installs from YouTube referrals.** Track with a UTM on the link in bio.
4. **Watch time on compilations.** Signal that the long-form arc works.
5. *Not a KPI:* raw views. Don't chase them.

### 1.5 What does NOT belong here

- Anything where Erica is being explained instead of being herself.
- Veronica monologues about caregiving philosophy. (That belongs on the personal channel.)
- Crying content. Crisis content. "Hard day" content unless Erica wants it shown.
- Anything Erica would be embarrassed by at 40.

## 2. Veronica (personal channel)

### 2.1 Mission

Structural truth-telling for the woman building something quietly while the world doesn't notice. You are the architect of a household, multiple businesses, and full-time caregiving — 25 years in. The chart says your seat is the builder behind the build, not the inspirational face. The channel reflects that.

### 2.2 Content pillars (six)

| Pillar | The promise | Example titles |
|---|---|---|
| **Unseen Labor** | How a household + multiple businesses + full-time caregiving actually get run, structurally. | "The four operations that keep my week from collapsing"; "Caregiving has a P&L. Here's mine." |
| **Building Forward** | When family-of-origin isn't the foundation, you build one. | "What estrangement actually costs — and what it gives back"; "Performed harmony is a tax. Here's the line item." |
| **Caregiving as Operations** | Not martyrdom. Systems, handoffs, redundancies. | "The caregiver vacation plan most families don't have"; "Why my daughter's daily planner is a system, not an app." |
| **Vedic Timing in Practice** | Applied to real decisions. Not predictions, not woo. | "How I time a launch against my Saturn return"; "Muhurta for working mothers: a working example." |
| **25 Years In** | Marriage and parenting Erica — discernment, not lessons. | "What 25 years married has taught me about saying no to family"; "The discernment my 30-year-old self didn't have." |
| **The Quiet Empire** | Print shop, Etsy, the working mother as builder. | "The Etsy shop nobody knows is mine"; "Print-on-demand as a caregiving-compatible business." |

### 2.3 Format spec

- **Length:** 8–15 minutes. Not Shorts. Mercury in the 12th wants depth, not feed-grinding.
- **Aspect:** 16:9 horizontal.
- **Shot:** Static camera, plain backdrop, one light. You sitting and talking. No B-roll for the first 90 days — the voice is the product.
- **Hook:** First sentence is a structural claim, not a question. ("Caregiving has a P&L." Not "Have you ever wondered…")
- **Cadence:** One video every Tuesday morning. Saturn pace. Compounding.
- **Companion form:** A written carousel + long caption on Instagram/Substack for the same idea, posted Thursday. The YouTube video is the canonical version; the carousel is the breadcrumb.

### 2.4 KPIs

In priority order:
1. **Email list growth.** YouTube → newsletter is the conversion that matters.
2. **Comment depth.** Length and specificity of comments > number of comments.
3. **Repeat viewers across pillars.** Are people watching the second video?
4. **Print shop / Etsy / future-product conversions from channel referrals.** UTM everything.
5. *Not a KPI:* subscriber count. Wrong target.

### 2.5 What does NOT belong here

- Erica as the subject. Erica is mentioned in service of a structural point, never as the lesson.
- "Day in my life" vlogs. Wrong format for this voice.
- Hot takes on parenting/caregiving discourse. Not your seat.
- Anything that performs harmony you don't have.

### Day-31 review

Sit down for 30 minutes. Answer five questions:
1. Which Short got the most *returning* viewers (not the most views)?
2. Which long video got the longest average watch time?
3. Which comment made you say "that's the audience"?
4. What did you flinch about posting? (That's a signal, both directions — flinching at honesty = post more; flinching at exposure = pull back.)
5. What did filming feel like in your body? (If you dreaded it three weeks running, the format is wrong, not you.)

## 5. What this strategy is *not* trying to do

- Not trying to grow fast. Both channels are designed to compound, not spike.
- Not trying to make Erica famous. The point is dignity at scale, not fame.
- Not trying to monetize via ads. The Daily Planner, the newsletter, the print shop, the eventual courses — those are the products. YouTube is the seat at the table, not the storefront.
- Not trying to be everywhere. YouTube + one written companion platform (carousels/Substack). That's the stack.
